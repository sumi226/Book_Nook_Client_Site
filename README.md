 ****  BookNook – A Quieter corner for readers. ****

BookNook is an online book platform where readers can discover books, explore new arrivals, share their favorite books, and borrow books from the community. It provides a convenient way for book lovers to connect and enjoy reading.

  🌐 Live Website 

****  Client Side: https://booknookclientsite.vercel.app/

✨ Key Features

****  Browse Books: Explore a collection of books and discover interesting titles from different categories.

****  New Arrivals: Stay updated with newly added books and explore the latest additions to the platform.

****  Book Details: View detailed information about individual books before deciding to borrow or read them.

****  Share a Book: Share your favorite books with the BookNook community and help other readers discover them.

****  Borrow & Return Books: Borrow available books and return them after completing your reading.

****  User Authentication: Securely register and log in using email/password or Google authentication.

****  Responsive Design: Enjoy a user-friendly experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
